Common questions

The camera shows nothing.
Kodo needs camera access. Open the Settings app → Kodo and turn Camera on.

A scanned Wi-Fi code doesn't connect.
Make sure you are near the network. If iOS asks to join, tap Join. You can also copy the password from the details screen.

What is free, and what needs Kodo Pro?
Scanning, history and plain QR codes are free. Templates, frames, extra colours, logos, barcodes, PDF and SVG export and batch scanning are Kodo Pro. You can try Pro features 5 times for free.

How do I restore Kodo Pro on a new iPhone?
With the same Apple Account: open Kodo → Settings → Kodo Pro and tap Restore purchases. If you bought Pro while signed in to a Kodo account, just sign in to that account on the new iPhone.

How do I cancel Kodo Pro?
Open the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Kodo, or tap Manage subscription inside Kodo Pro. Pro stays unlocked until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting your Kodo account does not cancel it.

Where is my history stored?
On your iPhone. If you sign in and turn on sync (Kodo 1.1 and later), it is also kept in your Kodo account so it appears on your other phones. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my account?
In Kodo, open Settings → Account → Delete account. The account and everything synced with it are deleted for good; the history on your iPhone stays.
