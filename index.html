Common questions

The camera shows nothing.
Kodo needs camera access. Open the Settings app → Kodo and turn Camera on.

A scanned Wi-Fi code doesn't connect.
Make sure you are near the network. If iOS asks to join, tap Join. You can also copy the password from the details screen.

How do I restore Kodo Pro on a new iPhone?
Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Kodo → Settings → Kodo Pro and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel Kodo Pro?
Open the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Kodo, or tap Manage subscription inside Kodo Pro. Pro stays unlocked until the end of the period you paid for.

Where is my history stored?
Only on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
